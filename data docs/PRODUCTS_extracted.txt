Managed Wi-Fi is a professionally managed wireless networking solution where Airtel handles the design, deployment, monitoring, maintenance, and support of your Wi-Fi network. This allows businesses to focus on their core operations while ensuring reliable connectivity.
2. What is the difference between Managed and Unmanaged Wi-Fi?
In an unmanaged Wi-Fi setup, the business is responsible for installing, configuring, monitoring, and troubleshooting the network. With Managed Wi-Fi, Airtel takes care of the entire lifecycle of the network, including performance optimization, security updates, monitoring, and technical support.
3. How secure is a Managed Wi-Fi network?
Airtel Managed Wi-Fi includes enterprise-grade security features such as secure authentication, access control, encryption, network segmentation, threat protection, and continuous monitoring to safeguard users and business data from unauthorized access and cyber threats.
